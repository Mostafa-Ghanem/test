Overview

One brand. Multiple formats. One visual rhythm.

The Tiffany work spans static social content, profile material and commercial motion. Rather than display every asset produced, this case study shows how the visual language carries from one touchpoint to the next — from identity and tone, into social creative, then into motion.

Challenge

Stay recognizable while the content keeps changing.

Campaign content has to adapt quickly: different messages, different formats and different moments. The challenge was to create enough visual consistency for Tiffany to remain recognizable without making every piece feel identical.

Creative direction

A clean teal-led language with room to shift in mood.

The system moves between bright, open compositions and darker, more dramatic moments. Turquoise and teal carry recognition, while near-black and soft neutral surfaces give the work contrast for both static and motion-led content.
